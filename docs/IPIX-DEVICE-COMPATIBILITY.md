# IPIX Rust integration and device compatibility catalogue

This document is the compatibility inventory and Rust-library map for the proposed ImpossibleEngine Physical Interface & XR System (IPIX).

- [Architecture, device catalogue, crate mapping, capability model, safety and roadmap](PHYSICAL-INTERFACE-XR.md)

The catalogue is deliberately organized by device family and integration protocol. It covers representative commercial products and open standards, but cannot truthfully enumerate every SKU, firmware revision, regional variant or future device. A product is not "supported" merely because its USB/Bluetooth identity can be detected.

See also [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) and [Platform variants](PLATFORM-VARIANTS.md).
